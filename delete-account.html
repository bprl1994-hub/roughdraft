Delete your Rough Draft account

Rough Draft, by Benjamin Lemon

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Rough Draft and sign in.
  2. On the Leagues screen, tap the account icon at the top right to open Account.
  3. Scroll down, tap Delete Account and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

Option 2: without the app

If you no longer have the app, email benjamin.lemon94@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject Delete my Rough Draft account.

We will confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner. You do not need to reinstall the app.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and email address
  • Your profile: display name, profile photo and nationality flag
  • Your notifications, notification settings and device tokens
  • Pending join requests and your Unlimited Leagues record in the app

What is kept, and why

  • Your past teams and scores stay in the history of leagues you played in, so other members' standings still add up. Your name, photo and flag are removed and you are shown as "Deleted player".
  • If you owned a league with other members, ownership passes to the longest-standing member. If you were the only member, the league is closed.
  • Apple or Google keep their own record of any purchase you made, under their policies. Uninstalling or deleting your account does not refund a purchase.

Full details are in the Privacy Policy.
