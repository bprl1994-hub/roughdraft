Rough Draft

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. Who we are

Rough Draft is a fantasy golf app operated by Benjamin Lemon, an individual based in Scotland ("we", "us").

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who we share it with and your rights under UK data protection law, including the UK GDPR.

Contact: benjamin.lemon94@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

  • Account details: email address, display name and password (passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication and never seen by us)
  • Profile details you choose to add: profile photo and nationality flag
  • Gameplay data: leagues you join or run, team selections, transfers, captains, scores and rankings
  • Notifications: a device token so we can send push notifications, plus your notification settings
  • Purchases: whether you have bought Unlimited Leagues. Payment is handled by Apple or Google. We never see your card or bank details
  • Technical data: device type, app version, IP address and diagnostic logs

We do not knowingly collect special category (sensitive) data.

3. How we use it

  • To create and run your account
  • To run leagues, score teams and show leaderboards to other players in your leagues
  • To send notifications you have switched on, such as draft reminders and results
  • To unlock and restore purchases
  • To keep the app secure, fix problems and improve it

4. Lawful basis

  • Contract: most processing is needed to provide the app you signed up for
  • Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention and improving the app
  • Consent: push notifications, which you can turn off at any time in the app or your device settings

5. Who can see your data

Other players in your leagues can see your display name, photo, nationality flag, teams and scores. League owners can see join requests.

We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google Firebase (hosting, authentication, database, notifications)
  • Apple App Store and Google Play (purchases and app distribution)
  • RevenueCat (managing purchase status)

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

6. International transfers

Some providers process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on safeguards approved under UK law, such as standard contractual clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account is open. If you delete your account, we delete your profile, photo, notifications, notification settings and device tokens. Your past teams and scores stay in the history of leagues you played in, so other members' results still add up, but your name, photo and flag are removed and shown as "Deleted player". Limited records may be kept where the law requires, for example purchase records held by Apple or Google.

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, and you can object to some processing. You can delete your account at any time from the Account page.

To make a request, email benjamin.lemon94@gmail.com.

9. Children

Rough Draft is not intended for anyone under 13. If we learn we hold data about a child under 13, we will delete it.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No online service can be completely secure.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please contact us first. You can also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

12. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
